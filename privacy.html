Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

VideoFactory ("the app") is a desktop application that runs locally on its owner's computer. It is not a web service and has no servers of its own. This policy explains what data the app accesses and what it does with it.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By connecting a YouTube channel you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

Data the app accesses

  • Google account authorization (OAuth tokens) for the YouTube channels the owner chooses to connect. Scopes are used only to upload videos, set thumbnails, and read the connected channel's name and ID.
  • Public YouTube data: search results and public statistics (titles, view counts, publish dates, channel subscriber counts) used to suggest video topics.

How data is used and stored

  • All data is stored only on the owner's own computer. OAuth tokens are stored encrypted with a key kept in the macOS Keychain.
  • The app does not collect, sell, or share personal data, and sends nothing to the developer.
  • Data is sent to Google/YouTube only to perform actions the owner requests (uploading a video, setting a thumbnail, reading statistics).
  • To generate original video ideas and scripts, the app may send text prompts — including public video titles from YouTube search results — to the AI service the owner has configured. No OAuth tokens or private account data are sent to AI services.
  • Public YouTube statistics are cached locally for at most 30 days and then refreshed or deleted.
  • No cookies or tracking technologies are used, and no data from other users is collected.

Revoking access and deleting data

You can revoke the app's access to your Google account at any time at Google security settings. Disconnecting a channel inside the app, or deleting the app's data folder (~/VideoFactory), permanently removes all stored tokens and data.
