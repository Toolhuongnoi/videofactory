VideoFactory

VideoFactory is a private desktop application that runs only on its owner's own computer. It helps the owner produce original educational videos for their own YouTube channels: it drafts a script, generates a voice-over, assembles licensed stock images into a video, and lets the owner review every video before anything is uploaded.

How it uses YouTube

  • Uploads videos the owner has reviewed and approved to the owner's own YouTube channels (private or scheduled), and sets their thumbnails.
  • Reads basic information about the owner's own channels (name, ID) to confirm which channel it is connected to.
  • Searches public YouTube videos and reads their public statistics (views, publish date) to suggest topics that are currently popular. It never downloads or re-uploads other people's videos.

VideoFactory is not offered to the public and has no user accounts or servers. Each channel is connected by its owner through Google's standard sign-in screen.
